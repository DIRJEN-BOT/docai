About DocAI Verify

DocAI Verify is an income verification API built for Indonesian fintech. It takes PDF bank e-statements (BCA, Mandiri) and extracts structured data — transaction histories, salary detection, income consistency scoring, and fraud signals — in milliseconds, without using any LLM or AI model.

The goal is simple: give lenders and fintech companies a fast, deterministic, and affordable way to verify a borrower's income from their bank statements. No black boxes, no per-request AI costs, no data retention.

Built By

DocAI Verify is built by a solo founder in Jakarta, Indonesia with 7+ years of software engineering experience in fintech. The product is designed, built, and maintained independently — from the parser engine to the API layer to this website.

If you're building lending products, BNPL, or any service that needs income verification, I'd love to hear how DocAI Verify can help.

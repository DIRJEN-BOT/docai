# Outreach Sequence: Investree

## 1. Company Overview

Investree is a P2P lending platform focused on invoice financing and working capital loans for Indonesian businesses. They verify cash flow from business bank statements to assess creditworthiness, but their current process is manual and time-consuming. Investree serves businesses that need quick access to capital, making speed and accuracy critical to their value proposition.

## 2. Specific Pain Point

Investree's business model relies on verifying cash flow from business bank statements to approve invoice financing and working capital loans. Their risk team manually reviews BCA and Mandiri statements to assess income patterns — a process that's slow and vulnerable to fraud. With invoice financing, the speed of verification directly impacts deal velocity. DocAI Verify automates this: we extract structured income data from bank statements in 30-150ms with built-in fraud detection, so Investree's risk team can approve loans faster and with more confidence.

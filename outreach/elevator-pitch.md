## The Pitch

**Problem:** Indonesian fintech lenders spend 30–60 minutes manually reviewing each bank statement PDF — or pay $0.50–$2 per verification through enterprise tools like Perfios. At 1,000 applications/month, that's either a team of analysts or Rp 32 juta in vendor fees.

**Solution:** DocAI Verify is a single API call. Upload a BCA or Mandiri e-statement PDF, get back verified monthly income, consistency score, and fraud flags in under 100ms. No bank linking, no LLM costs, no human in the loop.

**Proof:** BCA + Mandiri parsers live today. 50–100ms response time. Balance mismatch detection catches fabricated statements. Rp 1,000 per verification vs Rp 50,000–100,000 manual.

**CTA:** Free tier — 100 verifications/month, no credit card. Sign up and test with your own statements in 2 minutes.

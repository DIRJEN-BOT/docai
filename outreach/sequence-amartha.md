# Outreach Sequence: Amartha

## 1. Company Overview

Amartha is a leading P2P lending platform focused on MSMEs and rural borrowers in Indonesia. They leverage open banking data (Brick/Ayoconnect) for bank data access, but face a critical gap when borrowers provide PDF bank statements instead of API-connected accounts. Their rural focus means many borrowers cannot easily link their accounts digitally.

## 2. Specific Pain Point

Amartha uses Brick/Ayoconnect for bank data, but a significant portion of their rural borrowers provide PDF bank statements as fallback — and their team verifies these manually. This creates a dual-track system: API-connected borrowers are fast, but PDF-submitted borrowers create a bottleneck. DocAI Verify bridges this gap: we extract structured data from PDF bank statements (BCA + Mandiri) in 30-150ms with built-in fraud detection, so every borrower gets the same speed and accuracy regardless of how they submit their data.

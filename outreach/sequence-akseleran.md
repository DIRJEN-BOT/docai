# Outreach Sequence: Akseleran

## 1. Company Overview

Akseleran is a P2P lending platform focused on MSMEs and working capital loans in Indonesia. They provide financing to small businesses that often lack formal credit histories, relying on manual credit scoring based on bank statement analysis. Akseleran's growth depends on processing more loan applications faster without increasing risk.

## 2. Specific Pain Point

Akseleran's credit team manually reviews BCA/Mandiri bank statements to score MSME borrowers for working capital loans. This manual process is slow, inconsistent across analysts, and susceptible to document fraud. As Akseleran scales, their UMKM credit scoring pipeline needs automation to handle volume without sacrificing accuracy. DocAI Verify automates this: we extract structured income data from bank statements in 30-150ms with built-in fraud detection, giving Akseleran consistent, auditable credit scores for every applicant.

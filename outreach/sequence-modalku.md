# Outreach Sequence: Modalku (Pintar)

## 1. Company Overview

Modalku (now branded as Pintar) is Indonesia's largest P2P lending platform, focused on MSME lending with a reach across urban and semi-urban markets. They have processed billions in loan volume and are a market leader in Indonesia's P2P ecosystem. Their credit team currently relies on manual verification of bank statements, creating a bottleneck in their approval pipeline.

## 2. Specific Pain Point

Modalku's tim credit team manually verifies BCA bank statements uploaded by MSME borrowers — a process that is slow, error-prone, and susceptible to document fraud. With growing loan volume, this manual step creates a critical bottleneck in their approval cycle. Their risk team needs real-time, structured data extraction from bank statements to accelerate credit decisions without sacrificing fraud detection. DocAI Verify eliminates this manual step: borrower uploads a PDF, we return structured income data with fraud detection in 30-150ms.
